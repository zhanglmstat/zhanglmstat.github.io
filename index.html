About

A mathematician working at the intersection of risk and decision.

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Big Data and Statistics at Anhui University. My research centers on stochastic control problems — particularly dynamic optimization in insurance, reinsurance, and financial mathematics. I am interested in how agents make optimal decisions when faced with risk, ambiguity, and evolving market conditions.

Before joining Anhui University, I completed my Ph.D. in Statistics with a concentration in Actuarial Science and Financial Mathematics at East China Normal University, under the supervision of Prof. Rongming Wang and Prof. Jiaqin Wei. I also spent time as a Visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Waterloo, Canada, working with Prof. Bin Li.

My work has been published in journals including Scandinavian Actuarial Journal, Insurance: Mathematics and Economics, ASTIN Bulletin, and Journal of Computational and Applied Mathematics.

Research Interests

Where mathematics meets real-world risk.

01

Stochastic Control & Dynamic Optimization

Optimal decision-making under uncertainty using stochastic dynamic programming, Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equations, and game-theoretic approaches.

02

Reinsurance & Insurance Mathematics

Equilibrium reinsurance strategies, risk-sharing between insurers and reinsurers, and the design of optimal reinsurance contracts under mean-variance criteria.

03

Financial Mathematics & Investment

Portfolio optimization, asset allocation under regime-switching and ambiguity, and the interplay between investment and insurance decisions.
